# Global & Subsystem Business Invariants

## Global Economy
INV-MONEY-NON-NEGATIVE: Player balance must never drop below zero unless explicitly structured as a loan/bond.
INV-MONEY-NOT-NAN: Player money must never evaluate to NaN, null, or undefined in responses or UI.
INV-MONEY-CONSERVATION: Cash deductions and credits must match authorized transactions exactly with ledger balance changes.
INV-SIMBOOST-ACCURACY: SimBoosts balance must decrement only on valid unlock/speedup actions and never underflow.

## Inventory & Warehouse
INV-INVENTORY-NON-NEGATIVE: Warehouse resource quantities must never be negative.
INV-INVENTORY-CONSERVATION: Production input consumption and market order fulfillments must strictly decrement available stock.
INV-RESOURCE-COMPATIBILITY: Production inputs and retail sales must only accept resources valid for that building type.

## Building & Construction
INV-BUILDING-SLOT-LIMIT: Company cannot construct buildings on locked or non-existent slots.
INV-CONSTRUCTION-BUSY-GUARD: A building actively under construction or upgrade cannot initiate production or repeat upgrade requests.
INV-IDLE-RUSH-GUARD: Construction rush on an already completed/idle building must not charge cash or SimBoosts.

## Production Lifecycle
INV-PRODUCTION-IDEMPOTENCY: Finished production can only be collected once; duplicate collect requests must return 400/409 without double-crediting.
INV-PRODUCTION-INPUT-CHECK: Starting production must fail if required input materials or hourly cash wages are insufficient.

## Security & Auth
INV-AUTH-OWNERSHIP: Operations on company buildings, warehouse, orders, and executives must belong to the authenticated user session.
INV-SESSION-EXPIRATION: Expired sessions must be rejected with 401 Unauthorized.
INV-PERSISTENCE-AFTER-REFRESH: All committed game mutations must persist unchanged across browser refreshes and server reboots.
